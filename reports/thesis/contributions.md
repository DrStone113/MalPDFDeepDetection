# Contributions

1. Reproducible Python 3.11 research infrastructure with explicit dataset checksums, configurations, split manifests, environment metadata, and commit provenance.
2. A validated local RIT-PDFMal-2026 audit, feature dictionary/paper-alignment record, and terminology correction separating feature-equivalent vectors from PDF-file identity.
3. Two transparent evaluation protocols: paper-aligned row-level Protocol P and stricter feature-vector-disjoint Protocol G.
4. A fixed five-model classical baseline with explicit malicious-positive and macro metric semantics, runtime separation, and paper-comparison support.
5. A predeclared five-seed P–G robustness study that reports sample variability and paired direction without overstating statistical inference.
6. A separate paper-aligned XGBoost CV10 attempt with 20 recorded fits and out-of-fold integrity checks.
7. Verified static-feature extraction coverage, a 38-feature compatibility gate, and a separately versioned full-data static deployment artifact.
8. Native XGBoost TreeSHAP reporting and a fixed one-split MLP/FT-Transformer comparison, each bounded by their stated limitations.
9. Offline dynamic-report schema/routing infrastructure and an isolated CAPEv2 laboratory design that preserve a hard boundary against live malware execution.
